asset.** Don't wait until the last day.

## Code

- Written by the team in Java (Swing and AWT). No external libraries or engines.
- AI assistance: a large language model (Claude) helped with design, code, and documentation. The team reviewed,
  tested, and can explain the code, as described in [docs/03-code-guide.md](docs/03-code-guide.md).

## Research and references

- [USGS, The Philippines (2024 report)](https://www.usgs.gov/centers/national-minerals-information-center/philippines):
  Philippine mineral production.
- [MGB, Minerals Industry at a Glance (April 2025)](https://mgb.gov.ph/images/Mineral_Statistics/2024/MINERALS_INDUSTRY_AT_A_GLANCE_Updated_11_April_2025.pdf):
  Philippine mining statistics.
- [Wikipedia, Lapulapu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lapulapu): the history of Lapulapu.
- [Culture Trip, The Story of Lapu-Lapu](https://theculturetrip.com/asia/philippines/articles/the-story-of-lapu-lapu-the-legendary-filipino-hero):
  the stone-guardian legend.
- Folklore (Bakunawa, babaylan, tikbalang, engkanto): Philippine oral tradition, adapted respectfully for a fictional setting.
